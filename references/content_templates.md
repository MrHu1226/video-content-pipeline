# Content Generation Templates

## Timestamped Summary (content_summary.md)

### Structure

```markdown
# [视频标题]

> 来源: [URL]  |  时长: [HH:MM:SS]  |  日期: [YYYY-MM-DD]

## 核心要点

[3-5 句话概括视频的核心价值和主要论点]

## 内容概要

### [00:00 - 05:12] 主题一标题
[这一段的核心观点]
- 关键方法/框架: ...
- 举例: ...
- 启发/要点: ...

### [05:12 - 12:45] 主题二标题
...

## 关键收获

1. ...
2. ...
3. ...

## 对应片段

| 片段 | 时间段 | 主题 |
|------|--------|------|
| 01_opening_thesis.mp4 | 00:00 - 05:12 | ... |
| 02_case_study.mp4 | 05:12 - 12:45 | ... |
```

### Writing Guidelines
- Use Chinese throughout
- State each section's theme before listing details
- Extract actionable methods, not just descriptions of what was said
- Link to clip files when available
- When transcript quality is low, focus on topic flow over verbatim quotes

## WeChat Article (wechat_article.md)

### Structure

```markdown
# [吸引人的标题，不超过30字]

[开头段：用一个问题、故事或数据切入，1-2段，150字以内]

---

## [正文段落标题一]

[自然的叙述性段落，不是简单的要点罗列]

[每个段落讲清楚一个核心观点，配合具体的例子或数据]

## [正文段落标题二]

...

---

## 写在最后

[总结核心启发，给出行动建议或思考方向]
```

### Writing Guidelines
- Natural Chinese prose, NOT translated English
- Strong opening hook: question, surprising data, or relatable scenario
- Each section flows logically into the next
- Avoid bullet-heavy style — use paragraphs with clear topic sentences
- Technical concepts should include analogies or concrete examples
- Closing should consolidate actionable takeaways
- Total length: 1500-3000 characters for readability
- No emoji unless the topic is casual/lifestyle

## Xiaohongshu Posts (xiaohongshu_posts.md)

### Structure

```markdown
# 小红书内容 — [视频主题]

---

## 帖子 1: [标题，带 hook]

[正文，控制在 800 字以内]

[用简短段落，适当换行增加可读性]

#标签1 #标签2 #标签3 #标签4 #标签5

---

## 帖子 2: [标题]
...
```

### Writing Guidelines
- Generate 3-5 post options with different angles
- Title must be a hook: question, number, or bold claim
- Conversational tone, like sharing with a friend
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
- Include 5-8 relevant hashtags per post
- Each post under 1000 characters
- Different angles: tutorial, insight, opinion, list, story
